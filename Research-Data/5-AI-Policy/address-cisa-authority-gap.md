# From Compute to Congress: To Address CISA's Authority Gap, Reauthorize CISA 2015 and SLCGP

**Source:** dreadnode.io/research/from-compute-to-congress-to-address-cisas-authority-gap-reauthorize-cisa-2015-and-slcgp
**Author:** Daria Bahrami
**Date:** September 30, 2025

## Summary

Two critical cybersecurity programs — CISA 2015 and SLCGP — were set to expire September 30, 2025. Argues Congress must act to preserve voluntary information sharing, fund state/local security, and operationalize cyber operations as AI-powered threats advance.

## Relevance to This Workspace

Highlights the infrastructure gap that systematic AI security evaluation can help address through better threat intelligence sharing and coordinated defense.
