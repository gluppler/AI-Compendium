# Dreadnode's Policy Recommendations for the U.S. AI Action Plan

**Source:** dreadnode.io/research/policy-recommendations-us-ai-action-plan
**Author:** Daria Bahrami
**Date:** March 26, 2025

## Core Thesis

Two complementary priorities: leveraging AI to protect America (using AI for cybersecurity defense) and attacking AI to find its limits (red teaming, adversarial testing, vulnerability discovery). Both require systematic, reproducible evaluation frameworks.

## Key Recommendations

1. **Invest in offensive AI security** — Normalize and fund the development of AI systems that can probe, test, and stress-test other AI systems. Offense drives defense.

2. **Fund reproducible evaluation infrastructure** — The gap between proof-of-concept agents and production-ready security tools requires infrastructure for hosted evaluations, red teaming, optimization, training, and capability registries.

3. **Machine-readable compliance** — Move beyond static, manual compliance frameworks toward automated, continuous evaluation. FedRAMP 20x pilot proves this is technically feasible.

4. **Bridge the cyber-AI framework gap** — Cybersecurity and AI safety communities develop frameworks in isolation. Integration is essential — single frameworks like NIST Cyber AI Profile that bridge both domains.

5. **Accelerate SBIR/STTR for AI security** — Small business innovation funding has proven effective for vulnerability detection and OT security; expand to AI-specific security.

## Relevance to This Workspace

Provides policy justification for the systematic, reproducible evaluation methodology used throughout the workspace modules.
